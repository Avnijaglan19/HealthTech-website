How to Use Smart Workout Recommender

Your step-by-step guide to building workouts

Step 1: Login / Signup

Create an account or log in to access your personalized dashboard.

Step 2: Dashboard

View your workout summary including:
• Total workouts
• Total duration
• Weekly goal progress

Step 3: Start Workout

Click on "Start Workout" to begin creating your workout plan.

Step 4: Choose Method

Select how you want to build your workout:

Virtual Detection – Detect equipment using camera
Manual Input – Select preferences manually

Step 5: Follow Your Plan

Complete your workout and track your progress over time.

Track your Fitness Journey

After saving your workouts, visit the Progress page to see your total workouts, workour days, and workout types breakdown.

Tips for Best Results

  • Stay consistent with your workouts
  • Track your progress regularly
  • Try different workout types
  • Set realistic weekly goals
